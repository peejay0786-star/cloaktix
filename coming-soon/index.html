Private tickets on Midnight

CloakTix issues event tickets and passes as private NFTs on Midnight. Fans buy with a zero-knowledge proof made in their browser, resale stays under the organizer's price cap, and entry is a simple single-use QR code.

Follow @CloakTix on X

Demo on Midnight Preprod (test network)

  • Private by default

    Zero-knowledge proofs: buy and hold tickets without exposing your wallet or identity.

  • Fair resale

    Organizers set the resale cap per ticket type, enforced on-chain. Scalpers can't run prices up.

  • Simple QR entry

    Turn a ticket into a single-use QR pass at the door. Replays are refused.

  • 18+ without ID

    For age-restricted events, prove only "adult: yes". No ID document shown.
