Problem

Ticketing is broken twice

Scalping

Bots buy in bulk and resell at 3–10×. Organizers and fans lose; the resale rules can't be enforced.

Data exposure

To get in, fans hand over their name, email and sometimes an ID document. Each ticket becomes a data leak.

CloakTix: rules enforced on-chain, identity proven in zero knowledge.

2

Fan flow

Open a link, buy, show a QR

  • The organizer shares an event link; it opens on any phone
  • Buy with 1AM or Lace. The ZK proof is generated in the browser
  • At the door: "Show entry QR" burns the ticket on-chain and returns a single-use pass
  • The gate admits it once and refuses a replay
3

Organizer controls

A resale policy for each ticket type

Capped

Default. Resale price ≤ cap, checked on-chain. Above the cap the listing is refused.

No resale

Listing fails. Check-in still works.

Unlimited

Open market, still with the 3% fee.

18+

A ZK age proof is required. The contract learns only "adult: yes".

No transfer circuit is exported, so the policy can't be bypassed. Age verification will use Midnight Passport once it launches. Demo uses a test issuer.

Business model

on every sale and every resale

5

One wallet, every device

Buy on PC, show it on your phone

  • Identity H(sk), with sk derived from a domain-separated wallet signature (if deterministic)
  • Fallback: a transfer code (QR) with a "never share" warning
  • Tickets are read from the chain, not from local storage
  • Existing users keep their key
