Problem

Ticketing breaks trust twice.

Uncontrolled resale

Scalping puts fans and organizers at odds. Rules written in terms of service are not enough.

Enforce the resale policy on-chain.

Unnecessary identity exposure

Getting into an event should not mean handing over a wallet address or an identity document.

Prove eligibility in zero knowledge.

CloakTix combines fair ticket rules with private proofs, paid in NIGHT on Midnight.

Multi-buy, one approval

Up to 4 tickets in a single intent.

  • Each ticket is proved on the device, then all buyTicket calls are merged into one intent before signing.
  • 1AM sees one NIGHT input set and asks for one approval.
  • All or nothing: if the transaction fails, nothing is bought.
  • Fixes error 227 (two intents spending the same NIGHT coin). Confirmed live with 1AM on 9 Nov.

Rules and business model

Resale rules belong to the organizer.

Capped

Resale cannot exceed the organizer maximum.

No resale

Listing is refused. Entry still works.

Unlimited

No price cap. The fee still applies.

3%

On every sale and resale, set on-chain (feeBps 300).

97% organizer

Prices in USD, paid in NIGHT. No payout addresses shown.
